Upload Dutchie report

Use the same sales report as the ATV contest. It needs Budtender Name, Order ID, Total Inventory Sold and Net Sales columns. Upload yesterday's report each morning. Re-uploading a day replaces it. To credit attributions, also choose the attribution export, either at the same time or right after.

Kiosk sales / attributions

A budtender gets credit when the customer they logged buys the same day, matched by customer name in the Dutchie report. Credit goes to the person on the attribution, even if someone else rang the sale. Choose the attribution export and the Dutchie report together in Upload Dutchie report above. Uploading the full export again is fine: saved days are replaced and your review choices are kept. Blank names and test entries are skipped, and the same purchase only counts once.
